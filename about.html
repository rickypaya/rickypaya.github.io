Meet our leading dentists

Our dentists bring decades of experience and unwavering commitment to excellence. As the official team dentists for the University of Miami and FIU, they provide world-class care.

MLV

Dr. Marco Leyte-Vidal, DMD

Born in Havana Cuba, and raised in Miami, Florida, Dr. Marco Leyte-Vidal has earned a Bachelor of Science in Chemistry from the University of Miami in 1982 and a Doctor of Dental Medicine from the University of Florida College of Dentistry in 1986.

Alongside his private practice, he spent five years as an Assistant Professor in the Division of Oral and Maxillofacial Dentistry at the University of Miami School of Medicine.

A past two-term President of the Riviera Dental Study Club and a Fellow of the Academy of Sports Dentistry, Dr. Leyte-Vidal is also an active member of the American Dental Association, Florida Dental Association, South Florida District Dental Association, and International Team for Implantology.

MR

Dr. Michael Robinson, DMD

Dr. Michael Robinson was born and raised in Coventry, Rhode Island, and moved to Miami in 1987 to attend the University of Miami, where he earned a Bachelor of Science in Biology.

He received his Doctor of Dental Medicine from the University of Pennsylvania School of Dental Medicine in 1995 and returned to Miami to enter private practice.

Dr. Robinson is an active member of the American Dental Association, Florida Dental Association, South Florida District Dental Association, International Team for Implantology, and Academy for Sports Dentistry.

Alongside Dr. Leyte-Vidal, he serves as team dentist for the University of Miami Hurricanes and the Florida International University Panthers.
